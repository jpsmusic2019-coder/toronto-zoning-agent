              How the Numbers Are Made

              Every count, distance and date comes from fixed rules applied to those records. The approval rate counts approved and refused decisions only; deferred, withdrawn and undecided files are listed but not counted. Precedent is Strong at 80% or more approved, Mixed at 50–79%, Weak below 50%, and too few to call below 3 decided files.

              Project type comes from the City's own application sub-type (new house or addition), refined by keywords for pools, decks, legalizations and severances. Storeys are read from new-house descriptions. Time to hearing is a median, from filing to hearing. A variance counts as built when a building permit's description cites its decision number.
